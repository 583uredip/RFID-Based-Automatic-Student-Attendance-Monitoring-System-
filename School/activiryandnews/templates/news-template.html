KCHS Students Excel in SSC Result 2026

Kapotaksha Secondary High School achieves an outstanding 98% pass rate in the SSC Examination 2026, with 42 students securing GPA 5.0.

Teachers and students celebrating the SSC result

We are proud to announce that Kapotaksha Secondary High School achieved exceptional results in the SSC Examination 2026. The school recorded an overall pass rate of 98%, with 42 students securing the coveted GPA 5.0.

This achievement reflects the dedication of our students, the tireless effort of our teachers, and the continued support of parents and guardians throughout the academic year.

Category Result
Total Students Appeared120
Passed118 (98%)
GPA 5.042
GPA 4.0 and above75

The Principal of Kapotaksha Secondary High School congratulated all the students and urged them to continue their academic journey with the same dedication and commitment.

"This result is a testament to the hard work of our students and the commitment of our teachers. We aim to do even better next year." | Headmaster, Kapotaksha Secondary High School
