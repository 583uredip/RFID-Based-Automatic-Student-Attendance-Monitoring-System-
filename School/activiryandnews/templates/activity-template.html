Inter-School Science Fair | 2026

Kapotaksha Secondary High School students showcased their scientific talent at the District Inter-School Science Fair held on 15th August 2026.

Students presenting their projects at the Science Fair

On 15th August 2026, Kapotaksha Secondary High School proudly participated in the District Inter-School Science Fair. Students from classes 8 to 10 presented a wide range of innovative projects covering topics from renewable energy to environmental science.

The event was judged by a panel of experts from local universities and government science bodies. Our students demonstrated exceptional creativity and scientific understanding, earning recognition from the judges.

Highlights

  • 12 student projects were submitted from Kapotaksha Secondary High School.
  • 3 projects were shortlisted for the national round.
  • Best Project Award received by Class 10 students.

The school management congratulates all participating students and thanks the teachers who guided and mentored them throughout the preparation process.
