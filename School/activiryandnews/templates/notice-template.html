Annual Exam Schedule – 2026

Important information regarding the upcoming annual examination for all classes at Kapotaksha Secondary High School.

The annual examination for all classes will commence from 1st August 2026. All students are required to be present on time with their admit cards.

Students are advised to collect their admit cards from their respective class teachers by 28th July 2026. Attendance in all examinations is compulsory as per school policy.

Class Start Date Venue
Class 6 – 81st August 2026Main Hall
Class 9 – 102nd August 2026Exam Block A

For any queries, please contact the school office during working hours (9 AM – 2 PM, Saturday–Thursday).
