Durga Puja Festival

On 13 April 2026, Kapotaksha Secondary High School (KCHS) successfully organized the Durga Puja Festival on campus. The grand celebration honoured Goddess Durga and brought the community together in a vibrant expression of devotion and cultural heritage.

Event Highlights

  • Traditional puja rituals and offerings
  • Cultural performances and classical dance
  • Dhak drumming and festive music
  • Prasad distribution and community dinner

Durga Puja is one of the most cherished celebrations of KCHS, bringing together students, faculty, and the wider community in a shared spirit of devotion and festivity.
