📅 March 3, 2026 Festival

Holi Festival 2026

On Tuesday, 3rd March 2026, AIUB Hindu Society (AHS) successfully organized the Holi Festival on campus. The festival of colours was celebrated with immense joy and enthusiasm, uniting students from all backgrounds in a burst of vibrant festivity.

Event Highlights

  • Colourful Holi celebrations with organic colours
  • Music and dance performances
  • Traditional sweets and refreshments
  • Community bonding across departments

The event was a joyful expression of the spirit of Holi — spreading happiness, forgiveness, and the triumph of good over evil.
