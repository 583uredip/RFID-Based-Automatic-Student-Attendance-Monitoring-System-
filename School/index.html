ABOUT

Kapotaksha Secondary High School

Kapotaksha Secondary High School is a well-recognized secondary educational institution located in Khulna, Bangladesh. Established in 1995, the school is registered under the Directorate of Secondary and Higher Education (DSHE) with EIIN 117204. It is affiliated with the Jessore Education Board and is dedicated to providing quality education in a disciplined and student-friendly environment.

The institution offers secondary-level education with academic programs in Science, Business Studies, and Humanities. As a government-recognized school, it follows the national curriculum and emphasizes academic excellence, moral values, leadership, and extracurricular activities to ensure the all-round development of its students.

Kapotaksha Secondary High School continues to serve the local community by preparing students for higher education and responsible citizenship. Through experienced teachers, modern teaching methods, and a supportive learning atmosphere, the school strives to develop knowledgeable, skilled, and confident individuals who can contribute positively to society.

Why Choose Kapotaksha Secondary High School?

We provide quality secondary education through experienced teachers, a structured curriculum, and a strong focus on academic excellence to help students achieve outstanding results.

Our school offers a safe and friendly learning environment with modern teaching methods that encourage creativity, critical thinking, discipline, and lifelong learning.

Along with academics, students participate in sports, cultural programs, science fairs, and extracurricular activities that develop leadership, confidence, teamwork, and social responsibility.
