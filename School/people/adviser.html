ADVISERS

AIUB Hindu Society is approved by the Office of Student Affairs (OSA) of American International University – Bangladesh (AIUB). Throughout the entire journey, advisers of AHS formulate the foundation of the society and that foundation grows stronger with each executive body. Being the advisers of AHS, they mentor all activities of the society. They serve the society and its members with their idea proposals, advice, and experience whenever required.
