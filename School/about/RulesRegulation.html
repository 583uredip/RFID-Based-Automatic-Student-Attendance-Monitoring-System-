Rules & Regulation

Kapotaksha Secondary High School | Student Guidelines

SCHOOL MANAGEMENT

Kapotaksha Secondary High School is managed by a dedicated team of educational professionals and administrators who work together to maintain academic excellence, discipline, and the overall development of students. The school's administration consists of the following:

  • Managing Committee
  • Head Teacher
  • Assistant Head Teacher
  • Teaching Staff
  • Office Administration
  • Support Staff
  • Guardian Representatives

ADMISSION

  • Admission is open to eligible students who meet the school's admission requirements and available seat capacity.
  • Applicants must submit a completed admission form along with the required academic documents and birth certificate.
  • Students may be required to attend an admission test or interview, depending on the class and school policy.
  • Admission is confirmed only after verification of documents and payment of the prescribed admission fees.
  • Students and guardians are expected to follow the school's rules, regulations, and code of conduct.

VISION & MISSION

Kapotaksha Secondary High School is committed to providing quality education that inspires students to achieve academic excellence, develop strong moral values, and become responsible citizens. Our mission is to create a safe, inclusive, and supportive learning environment where every student can discover their potential, build leadership skills, and prepare for future success.

STUDENT CODE OF CONDUCT

  • Students must attend school regularly and arrive on time.
  • Students must wear the prescribed school uniform during school hours.
  • Respect teachers, staff, classmates, and school property at all times.
  • Maintain discipline and follow all classroom and school rules.
  • Bullying, harassment, fighting, or any form of misconduct is strictly prohibited.
  • Students must complete homework and participate actively in academic activities.
  • Mobile phones and other electronic devices may only be used with school permission.
  • Students should participate in school programs, cultural activities, and sports whenever encouraged.
  • Any damage to school property must be reported immediately.
  • Violation of school rules may result in disciplinary action as determined by the school authority.

GENERAL RULES

  • Students must keep the school campus clean and protect school property.
  • Parents or guardians should inform the school in case of prolonged absence.
  • Students must obtain permission before leaving the school campus during school hours.
  • Cheating during examinations or assignments is strictly prohibited.
  • Respect the cultural, religious, and social diversity of the school community.
  • Follow all safety instructions during laboratory classes, sports, and school events.
  • Maintain proper behavior both inside and outside the classroom.
  • Any form of violence, vandalism, or substance abuse is strictly forbidden.
  • The school authority reserves the right to amend rules whenever necessary.

ELECTION

  • If any position on the Committee becomes vacant, the Committee can call a General Meeting for the election of a member to that position.
