Intimidation Policy

Kapotaksha Secondary High School — Safe Space Commitment

One of KCHS's top priorities is to safeguard a healthy environment for its students. KCHS is committed to the values of civility, equity, respect, non-discrimination and an appreciation of diversity. It is imperative that situations of discrimination, intimidation, harassment, and assault stop as soon as possible and that we prevent those from happening again. This policy addresses ways to prevent harassment, as well as where to find support and redress when harassment occurs.

Definitions

Discrimination

The act of making distinctions between human beings based on the groups, classes, or other categories to which they are perceived to belong.

Intimidation

Intentional behavior that "would cause a person of ordinary sensibilities" to fear injury or harm.

Harassment

All forms of repeated or ongoing unwelcome and distressing conduct, including but not limited to sexual harassment, psychological harassment, or harassment based on a ground prohibited by law.

Assault

The act of inflicting physical harm or unwanted physical contact upon a person or, in some specific legal definitions, a threat or attempt to commit such an action.

What should I do if I think I am a victim?

  • Speak directly to the person involved — identify the behavior clearly and ask them to stop and correct it.
  • Discuss the situation with the Head Teacher and help them ensure justice is served.

What if I am harassed by a student?

If you are being discriminated against, intimidated, harassed, or assaulted by an KCHS student who is not related to the School — speak directly to the person, identify the behavior, and ask them to stop. If this does not work, file an official complaint with OSA against the student.

What if I am harassed by a school student?

File an oral or official complaint with the Head Teacher. The Head Teacher will formally warn the student. If the incident recurs after the warning, inform the Head Teacher again with evidence. The Head Teacher will take further action and, if necessary, notify OSA.

What if I am harassed by an alumni member?

File an oral or official complaint with the Head Teacher. The Head Teacher will consult with advisors and formally warn the alumni member. If the incident recurs, inform the Head Teacher with evidence. The Head Teacher will take further action and, if necessary, notify OSA.

What happens if someone is proven guilty?

If a student or alumni member is proven guilty, the school authority will ensure the following consequences:

  • Banned from all future KCHS events.
  • Formally warned not to repeat the behavior.
  • The victim retains the right to file an official complaint under Bangladesh law.

If the person is a non-student of KCHS, action will be taken according to KCHS's own policy.

Conclusion

It is the duty of all members to uphold this policy. This is a serious matter and will be handled with strict zero-tolerance. Once a member has been proven guilty and banned, all ties with that individual are severed, and the club holds no further responsibility regarding their actions.
